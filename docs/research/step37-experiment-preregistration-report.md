## Scientific question and hypotheses

On an independent, source-complete cohort (STEP 36; Gulf Stream box, 2010-07..2010-10), does (Q1) the ocean temporal representation of the frozen HYCOM baseline (native 3-hourly vs UTC-daily mean) materially change 72 h drifter-trajectory agreement, and (Q2) does the frozen candidate (GLORYS12V1 15.810070 m + WW3 Stokes x 1.0 + NCEP-R2 windage alpha 0.002) reproduce lower observed-vs-model position error than the frozen HYCOM baseline?

H1 (primary): Paired 72 h M3 error of the candidate minus the HYCOM baseline has a negative median across the cohort AND the candidate has lower error in at least 2/3 of non-tied drifter pairs (CANDIDATE_DESCRIPTIVELY_FAVORED under the locked STEP 30A rule). Null: H1-null: the locked rule yields NO_CLEAR_DESCRIPTIVE_DIFFERENCE or HYCOM_DESCRIPTIVELY_FAVORED. Expected winner: NONE DECLARED.
H2 (temporal): Paired 72 h M3 error of HYCOM_DAILY minus HYCOM_NATIVE_3H has a non-negative median with >= 2/3 consistency (NATIVE_3H_DESCRIPTIVELY_FAVORED) - i.e. temporal representation matters. (secondary, preregistered; same metric family)
H3 (structural): The H1 label is unchanged when alpha = 0 (structural pair) is substituted for alpha = 0.002 in both models. (exploratory robustness; never replaces H1)
H4 (horizon): The sign of the H1 median delta is the same at 24 h and 48 h. (exploratory)

## Statistical hierarchy, primary aggregation and bootstrap (pre-lock clarification, protocol v1.1)

LEVEL 1: window / cluster (9 STEP 36 windows; shared forcing period, release time and regional flow state). LEVEL 2: drifter pair nested within its window (20 drifters; one candidate-vs-baseline pair per drifter per horizon). LEVEL 3: trajectory evaluation points (900 s output samples and the 24/48/72 h evaluation instants) nested within a drifter pair. trajectory evaluation points are NEVER treated as independent statistical observations for the primary inference; the primary inference unit is the window; drifter pairs are observations nested within windows; pooled drifter-level statistics are descriptive.

Notation: V_w = set of drifters i in window w with a valid 72 h pair (candidate position, baseline position and observation all exist at exactly t0+72h); delta_i = E_C,i(72h) - E_A,i(72h) in km; W* = set of windows with |V_w| >= 1. Window statistic: Delta_w = median_{i in V_w}(delta_i)  (median of the valid drifter-pair deltas within window w; for an even |V_w| the arithmetic mean of the two middle values). Primary statistic: Theta = median_{w in W*}(Delta_w)  (median across the window-level medians; equal window weighting; a window with |V_w| = 0 is excluded from W* and counted as NOT_AVAILABLE, never imputed). Exact formula: Theta = median_{w in W*} [ median_{i in V_w} ( E_C,i(72h) - E_A,i(72h) ) ]. Weighting: equal-window weighting (each window contributes one Delta_w regardless of its drifter count); the pooled drifter-level median median_{i in union V_w}(delta_i) is reported as a secondary descriptive statistic for comparability with STEP 30A / STEP 32 and never replaces Theta

Window-level bootstrap (primary interval): for b = 1..B: draw exactly 9 windows from the 9 STEP 36 windows uniformly with replacement (numpy.random.default_rng(20260907), rng.integers(0, 9, size=9) per replicate, replicates drawn sequentially from the single generator); keep every valid drifter-pair delta belonging to each sampled window (a window sampled k times contributes its Delta_w k times); drifter pairs are never resampled independently for the primary interval; recompute the complete primary statistic with the identical nested rule: Theta_b = median over the sampled windows (with |V_w| >= 1) of Delta_w; store Theta_b; if a replicate contains no window with a valid pair it is recorded as NOT_AVAILABLE and reported (count), not silently dropped or redrawn. B = 10000; seed = 20260907. 95 % percentile interval = [Q_0.025, Q_0.975] of the stored Theta_b values (numpy.quantile with method 'linear' over the available replicates). Small-cluster limitation: the primary cluster count is 9 windows; the 95 % interval is an uncertainty estimate under the preregistered window-level resampling scheme and is NOT to be interpreted as establishing asymptotic normality or nominal coverage; the success criterion is not changed because of small n

Sensitivity only: drifter-level percentile bootstrap: resample the valid drifter pairs of the cohort with replacement (n = |union V_w|), B = 10000, same seed 20260907 on a separate default_rng instance, recompute the pooled median delta; 95 % percentile interval — ignores clustering; reported next to the primary window-level interval; can never replace the primary result or alter the success criterion.

Primary success criterion UNCHANGED: descriptive label CANDIDATE_DESCRIPTIVELY_FAVORED (locked STEP 30A pooled-pair rule) AND window-level 95 % interval of Theta entirely < 0 km. Consistency check: the label (pooled-pair rule) and Theta (nested statistic) are distinct, well-defined quantities; requiring both is a conjunction, not a contradiction; no inconsistency was found
