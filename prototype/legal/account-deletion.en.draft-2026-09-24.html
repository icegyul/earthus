Delete your EARTHUS account

App: EARTHUS · Developer: {{business name}} (sole proprietorship, Republic of Korea) · Contact: dalur@kakao.com

You can delete your EARTHUS account without installing the app, in either of the two ways below. C01

Option 1 — Delete it yourself (immediate)

This works the same in the Android app and in a web browser (https://earthus.net). C01

  1. Open https://earthus.net (or the app) and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Two confirmation dialogs appear. After you confirm both, the account is deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

Before deleting, you can use Download my data on the same screen to save your account details, consent records and optional usage events. C01 C02 C06

Option 2 — Ask us by e-mail

Use this if you cannot sign in or no longer have the app.

  • To: dalur@kakao.com
  • Subject: EARTHUS account deletion request
  • Body: the e-mail address you signed in with and the sign-in method (Google or Apple). Please send it from that account's e-mail address — we use it to confirm it is you. If you write from another address, we will send a confirmation to the account address.
  • Handling: after confirmation we delete the account within {{deadline — confirm the statutory period (e.g. 10 days)}} and reply with the result.
  • Never send passwords or verification codes. We will not ask for them.
